O problema

Uma campanha não tem um formato. Tem trinta.

Um filme de 15 segundos aprovado em 16:9 vira story, feed, painel de rua, empena, elevador, totem de aeroporto e mais uma dúzia de telas que ninguém tinha previsto. Cada uma com proporção própria.

O caminho tradicional é abrir a master, duplicar a comp, mudar o tamanho e arrastar camada por camada até caber. Multiplicado por trinta formatos, e refeito toda vez que o cliente pede um ajuste no filme original.

Sem o plugin

Cada formato é trabalho manual. Uma alteração na master significa refazer os trinta.

Com o KamaleOOH

Você marca cada camada uma vez dizendo como ela deve se comportar. O plugin gera todos os formatos a partir dessa regra, e regenera de novo quando a master muda.

O que ele não é

Não é um botão mágico que adivinha layout. Proporções muito distantes da master sempre vão pedir um ajuste fino. O plugin avisa quando esse é o caso, em vez de entregar algo torto em silêncio.

O modelo mental

Master marcada, formatos derivados

Só existem duas ideias no produto inteiro. Entendendo elas, o resto é interface.

1. A master é a fonte da verdade

Uma comp por família de proporção, nomeada MASTER_16x9, MASTER_9x16, MASTER_1x1, MASTER_STRIP_H ou MASTER_STRIP_V.

2. Cada camada carrega uma regra

Você seleciona a camada e clica em um dos botões de tipo. O plugin grava a regra no campo Comment e, ao gerar um formato, traduz isso em expressions de posição e escala.

Marcar camadas→ Gerar formato→ Ajustar se precisar→ Entregar

Passo 1

Marcar as camadas

Este é o único passo que exige critério seu. Tudo depois dele é automático.

Abra a master, selecione uma ou mais camadas e clique no tipo correspondente.

BotãoUse paraO que faz
ANCTexto, logo, CTA, seloMantém a distância fixa até a borda. Não distorce nem muda de tamanho.
DYNFundo fotográfico, vídeo de fundoPreenche o quadro preservando a proporção, cortando o excedente. Você escolhe o ponto focal.
STAGradiente, textura, cor chapadaEstica para preencher. Distorce, então use só onde a distorção não aparece.
STRIPCamadas em formatos alongadosProp, Left, Center ou Right.

Ponto focal do DYN

Ao marcar como DYN, escolha para onde o corte deve puxar: ← esquerda, · centro, → direita. Se o rosto está à esquerda do quadro, escolha esquerda, assim ele sobrevive ao corte em 9:16.

From Layer usa a posição de uma camada selecionada como ponto focal.

O erro mais comum: marcar produto ou pessoa como DYN. O DYN é para fundos que cobrem o quadro inteiro. Elemento posicionado vai de ANC, senão é cortado junto com o fundo.

A marcação fica no campo Comment da camada, como rf: mode=ANCHOR. Dá para conferir direto na timeline.

The problem

A campaign doesn't have one format. It has thirty.

A 15-second film signed off in 16:9 becomes a story, a feed post, a street panel, a building wrap, a lift screen, an airport totem, and a dozen more screens nobody planned for. Each with its own aspect ratio.

The usual route is to open the master, duplicate the comp, change the size and drag layers around until they fit. Multiplied by thirty formats, and redone every time the client tweaks the original film.

Without the plugin

Every format is manual work. One change to the master means redoing all thirty.

With KamaleOOH

You tag each layer once with how it should behave. The plugin derives every format from that rule, and regenerates again when the master changes.

What it isn't

It isn't a magic button that guesses layout. Ratios far from the master will always need a fine pass. The plugin tells you when that's the case instead of quietly shipping something crooked.

The mental model

Tagged master, derived formats

There are only two ideas in the whole product. Once they click, the rest is interface.

1. The master is the source of truth

One comp per ratio family, named MASTER_16x9, MASTER_9x16, MASTER_1x1, MASTER_STRIP_H or MASTER_STRIP_V.

2. Each layer carries a rule

Select a layer and click one of the type buttons. The plugin writes the rule into the layer's Comment field and, when generating a format, turns it into position and scale expressions.

Tag layers→ Generate→ Adjust if needed→ Deliver

The families

The plugin classifies any set of dimensions by its ratio, automatically:

Getting started

Installation

One installer, one panel. It handles the whole process: building masters, generating formats and wrapping up.

macOS

  1. Quit After Effects.
  2. Open KamaleOOH_Editors_Mac.pkg and follow the installer. It asks for your admin password.
  3. Launch After Effects.

Windows

  1. Quit After Effects.
  2. Run KamaleOOH_Editor_Windows.exe.
  3. Launch After Effects.

Security prompt on first launch

The installer isn't code-signed, so your system warns you before opening it. That's expected, and you only need to allow it once.

On macOS, if you see a message about an unidentified developer, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the notice about KamaleOOH and click Open Anyway. Then open the installer again.

On Windows, if you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Opening the panel

In After Effects: Window → Extensions → KamaleOOH Editor.

Installing over a previous version is safe. Your license stays active, so there's no need to re-enter the key.

Not showing up in the menu? Quit and relaunch After Effects. If it still doesn't appear, the installer likely didn't get admin permission. Run it again.

Access

License and trial

7-day free trial

The first time you open the panel, enter your name and email and click Email me a code. A 6-digit code arrives in your inbox: type it in the panel and click Start Free Trial. The trial unlocks the full plugin for 7 days, tied to that computer.

Activating a license

Your key arrives by email right after purchase, in the format KM-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. In the panel, click I have a license key, paste it and click Activate.

Step 1

Tag your layers

This is the only step that needs your judgement. Everything after it is automatic.

Open the master, select one or more layers and click the matching type.

ButtonUse forWhat it does
ANCText, logo, CTA, badgeKeeps a fixed distance to the edge. Never distorts or resizes.
DYNPhotographic or video backgroundFills the frame preserving ratio, cropping the overflow. You pick the focal point.
STAGradient, texture, flat colourStretches to fill. It distorts, so use it only for elements where that doesn't show.
STRIPLayers in very elongated formatsProp, Left, Center or Right.

DYN focal point

When tagging as DYN, choose which way the crop should pull: ← left, · centre, → right. If the subject's face sits on the left of the frame, choose left so it survives the 9:16 crop.

From Layer uses a selected layer's position as the focal point.

The most common mistake: tagging a product or a person as DYN. DYN is for backgrounds that cover the whole frame. Positioned elements go ANC, or they get cropped along with the background.

The tag lives in the layer's Comment field, as rf: mode=ANCHOR. You can check it straight from the timeline.

Step 2

Quick Convert

With the master open and layers tagged, the Quick Convert section instantly produces the most common conversions: 16:9 → 9:16, 9:16 → 16:9, 16:9 → Strip and 9:16 → Strip.

The new comp is named after the target family, 9x16 for instance, with the reflow already applied.

Fine-tuning

Every converted layer gets three controls in Effect Controls:

ControlEffect
Offset XShifts horizontally, in pixels
Offset YShifts vertically, in pixels
ScaleMultiplies the size, as a percentage

They're ordinary sliders, so keyframe them if the adjustment needs to change over time.

Step 3

Promote to Master

Once you've adjusted a conversion, you can turn it into the official master for that family. Click ↑ Promote to Master.

adjusted 9x16→clean MASTER_9x16
  • The comp is duplicated. The copy becomes MASTER_9x16, with your adjustments baked in and the sliders removed.
  • The original comp stays untouched, sliders and all, in case you want to go back.

If a MASTER_9x16 already exists, the previous one is renamed to MASTER_9x16_old1 rather than overwritten.

Step 4

Generate formats

One at a time

In the Format section, type width, height and fps (optional), click + to add it to the list, then Generate All.

Onto an existing comp

Apply to Active Comp applies the reflow to a comp you made yourself, instead of creating a new one.

Checking first

Check Deviation compares the open comp's ratio against the master's:

DeviationReadingWhat to expect
up to 18%CleanComes out ready, no touch-up
18% to 25%1-2 layer tweaksOne or two elements need a nudge
above 25%Manual adjustment neededWorth building a dedicated master

A high deviation isn't a flaw in the plugin: it's the real distance between the two ratios.

Step 5

CSV batch

For a whole campaign, list the formats in a spreadsheet and generate them in one go. Click Batch (CSV) and pick the file.

CSV
KamaleOOH_formats.csv 22 OOH and digital formats, pre-filled, with English column names. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets, adjust and save as CSV.

File format

Only width and height are required. Portuguese column names (largura, altura) work too.

dim,width,height,family,bucket,fps,duracao_seg
Feed,1080,1080,,,30,6
Story,1080,1920,,,30,6

ColumnRequiredWhat it does
width / larguraYesWidth in pixels

The sheet can have a title and a caption above the header row. The plugin finds the right line on its own. Comma or semicolon both work.

Where the comps land

FolderWhat goes there
FORMATS_16x9, FORMATS_9x16…Normally generated formats, split by family
FORMATS_ManualRows marked bucket = manual
FORMATS_ReviewNo master for that family. Derived from another one, needs a look

Anything in FORMATS_Review means a master is missing for that family. Build one with Promote to Master and run the batch again.

Step 6

Wrapping up

Bake & Lock

Bake & Lock freezes the open comp: expressions become fixed values and the controls are removed. The comp stops being responsive and becomes a result.

Freeze before delivering. While the controls are still there, the comp keeps responding to size.

Closing the campaign

With every format generated and checked, run Bake & Lock on each comp headed for render. From there it's standard After Effects: render queue or Media Encoder.
